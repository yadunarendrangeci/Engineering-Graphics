Development of surfaces

Watch the surface of a solid unfold into its flat pattern (right), next to the 2D development as drawn on paper (left).

2D development

Thick lines = outline you cut along; thin lines = fold lines (or generators on a cylinder); purple = dimensions. Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan, double-click to fit.

3D unfolding

Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, double-click to reset. The first lateral face stays fixed and every other face swings about its shared edge until the whole surface lies in one plane. "⟂ Development" looks straight at that plane, so the pattern is seen in its true shape.
