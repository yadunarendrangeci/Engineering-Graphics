Isometric projection of a cube

The isometric projection of a cube is its front view when it rests on a corner with a body diagonal perpendicular to VP. 2D construction on the left, 3D model on the right.

2D construction

Front views above XY, top views below. Dashed = hidden edges; red dash-dot = body diagonal; thin dotted = projectors. Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan, double-click to fit.

3D model

Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, double-click to reset. Blue = VP, teal = HP. The front view (blue) is drawn on VP and the top view (teal) on HP. At stage 3 click "Front (VP)": the cube looks exactly like its isometric projection, a regular hexagon.
