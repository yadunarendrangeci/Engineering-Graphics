3D: drag to rotate, scroll (or pinch) to zoom, double-click to fit. Teal = HP, blue = VP, orange = cutting plane, red = section. The front and top views are drawn on VP and HP. "⟂ Section" looks perpendicular to the cut face, so the section is seen in its true shape. In step 4 the purple AIP is parallel to the cut, perpendicular to VP, and meets VP along X1Y1. The section is projected onto it, then the AIP is swung about X1Y1 until it lies on VP. Choose "VT itself" to swing the section plane about its own trace VT instead. At 90°, click "Front (VP)" to see exactly the 2D drawing. Magenta dashes are distances from VP: from VP to each cut point, from XY to its top view on HP, and from the hinge to the true-shape point. All three are equal.
